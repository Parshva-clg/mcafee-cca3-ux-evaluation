Which protection is right for me?

Start with what you want to protect. Antivirus focuses on device security. Total Protection brings together device and identity essentials, while McAfee+ offers broader privacy, identity and device protection. Use the plan finder above, then check current Indian plan details on McAfee’s website.

Will it work on my devices?

Supported operating systems, device limits and features depend on your subscription. Check the system requirements and device coverage on the official plan page before purchasing. Not every feature is available on every device.

How does automatic renewal work?

After your initial subscription term, eligible subscriptions automatically renew annually at the renewal price in effect at that time, until you cancel. Introductory and renewal prices may differ. You can manage renewal settings in your McAfee account after purchase.

What is the money-back guarantee?

For an initial purchase, you may request a refund through McAfee Customer Support within 30 days. For automatic renewal of a term of one year or longer, the stated refund window is 60 days. Review the current offer and refund terms on McAfee’s website.

Can this page check my device’s security?

No. This is an independent redesign prototype. The dashboard, score, alerts and protection states are illustrative. No antivirus scan, identity monitoring or VPN connection is performed by this page.
